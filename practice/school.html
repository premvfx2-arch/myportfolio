Welcome to St. Georges Preparatory School

At St. Georges, we believe in nurturing the whole child. Our comprehensive approach to education combines rigorous academics with character development, arts, athletics, and community service.

Our state-of-the-art campus provides an inspiring environment where students discover their passions and develop the skills needed for success in an ever-changing world.

  • International Baccalaureate World School
  • STEM-focused curriculum with modern labs
  • Comprehensive arts and music programs
  • Competitive athletics and wellness programs
  • College counseling from Grade 9
